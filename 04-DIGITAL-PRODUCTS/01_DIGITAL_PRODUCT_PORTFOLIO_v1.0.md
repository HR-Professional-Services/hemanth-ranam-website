## 1. Overview & Instant Delivery Model
Digital products are self-serve, turnkey operational assets delivering immediate utility to founders and business operators. Upon Stripe checkout completion:
1. Webhook invokes `backend/Code.gs`.
2. Dedicated folder in Google Drive (`04 - DIGITAL PRODUCTS`) generates a unique view-only copy link for the purchaser's Google account.
3. Automated Gmail notification delivers download links, instructions, and video walkthroughs within 60 seconds.
