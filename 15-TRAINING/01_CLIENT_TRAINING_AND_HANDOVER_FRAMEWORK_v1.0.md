## 1. Handover Philosophy
A technical system is only successful if the client and their team can operate it independently with 100% confidence. Every project includes structured handover materials delivered directly into their private Google Drive folder (`04 - Training & SOPs`).

---

## 2. Standard Handover Deliverables Package
1. **Loom Video Walkthrough (5–15 mins):**
   - System architecture overview.
   - Live walkthrough of everyday workflows (adding leads, processing quotes, viewing dashboards).
   - How to manage common edge cases.
2. **Step-by-Step SOP (Standard Operating Procedure):**
   - High-contrast screenshot instructions.
   - Field definitions and required vs. optional fields.
   - Troubleshooting steps for failed webhooks or invalid data entries.
3. **Admin Credential & Environment Transfer:**
   - Transfer of ownership for Google Sheets, Apps Script projects, or Frappe instances.
   - Instructions on granting or revoking internal employee permissions.
4. **14-Day Post-Delivery Warranty:**
   - Direct asynchronous support via private communication channel to resolve any initial edge cases or minor questions.
