### 1. Architectural Strategy
- **Core Domain**: `hemanth.ranam.dev` / `scalenovasys.com`
- **Dynamic Sitemap**: Dynamically assembled at `/sitemap.xml` referencing all public canonical pages.
- **Robots Directives**: Standard `/robots.txt` granting crawling to search engines while protecting admin, test, and private checkout paths.
- **Canonical Tags**: Self-referencing canonical URLs on every indexable page preventing duplicate content issues.
- **OpenGraph & Twitter Cards**: High-resolution branded share cards on every public route.
- **JSON-LD Structured Data**:
  - `ProfessionalService` & `Person` schema on the root layout.
  - `Product` & `Service` schemas with fixed pricing models.
  - `BreadcrumbList` schemas across hierarchical directory pages.
  - `FAQPage` schemas for high-converting rich snippets.
