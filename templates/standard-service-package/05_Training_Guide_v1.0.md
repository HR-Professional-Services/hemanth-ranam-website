## 3. Knowledge Check & Practical Exercise
Before commencing daily operations, have your designated operator execute this test flow:
1. Submit a test enquiry via the web form with name `Test Lead`.
2. Verify that an alert arrives in your management email within 60 seconds.
3. Confirm that a new row appears in the spreadsheet with status `New`.
4. Update status to `Qualified` and verify that the KPI dashboard metrics update automatically.
