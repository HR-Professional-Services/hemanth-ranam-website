### 1. Digital Product Refund Policy
Due to the immediate access granted to downloadable files, master Google Sheets templates, and source code packages:
> Purchases of instant-download digital products, checklists, and code templates are non-refundable once download links or Google Drive access have been granted, subject to applicable mandatory consumer protection laws.

### 2. Implementation Services Refund Policy
For custom development, consulting, and automation services:
> Service agreements are milestone-based. A 50% deposit secures implementation capacity. If a project is cancelled prior to milestone sign-off, work delivered to date is billed at standard pro-rata rates and any unearned balance refunded. Once final deliverables are approved and handed over, fees are non-refundable.

### 3. Monthly Retainers Policy
> Monthly maintenance and support plans (Starter Care, Business Care, Systems Partner, Growth Partner) operate on a recurring monthly cycle. Clients may cancel at any time with 7 days' written notice before the next billing date. Services remain active through the end of the paid period.
