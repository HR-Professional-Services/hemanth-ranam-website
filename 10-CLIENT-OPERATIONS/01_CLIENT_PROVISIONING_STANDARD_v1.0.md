### 1. Client Identifier Standards
- **Client ID**: Format `CLI-YYYY-XXXX` (e.g. `CLI-2026-0042`). Unique per customer account.
- **Order ID**: Format `ORD-YYYY-XXXX` (e.g. `ORD-2026-0108`). Unique per transaction.
- **Workspace Name**: `[Client ID] - [Sanitized Company / Client Name]` (e.g., `CLI-2026-0042 - Acme Logistics`).

### 2. Standard Client Workspace Anatomy
Inside Google Drive: `HR - Services / 13 - CLIENT MANAGEMENT / [Workspace Name]`:
- `00 - Client Profile & Contracts`: Service agreement, scope sheets, credentials handover.
- `01 - Requirements & Intake`: Client intake form responses, business discovery notes.
- `02 - Working Files`: Draft spreadsheets, diagrams, development scratchpad.
- `03 - Deliverables`: Final signed PDFs, master spreadsheets, code packages.
- `04 - Training & SOPs`: Video walkthrough links, written user manual, operating blueprints.
- `05 - Support & Milestone Notes`: Meeting minutes, sprint reviews, SLA support tickets.

### 3. Support & SLA Tiers
- **One-Time Implementation**: 14 to 30 days priority warranty included.
- **Starter Care ($29/mo)**: 48-hour email response SLA, monthly uptime & form checkups.
- **Business Care ($79/mo)**: 24-hour email response SLA, 2 hours minor workflow adjustments/mo.
- **Systems Partner ($149/mo)**: 12-hour response SLA, bi-weekly health audits, direct architect access.
- **Growth Partner ($299/mo)**: 4-hour critical response SLA, continuous workflow evolution.
