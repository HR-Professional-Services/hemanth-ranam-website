## 1. Purpose & Scope
This directory houses deprecated documents, superseded code drafts, legacy marketing assets, and historical records. Nothing is deleted permanently without deliberate archival.

---

## 2. Retention Guidelines
- **Deprecated Specifications:** When a new major version of a document is published (e.g. `v2.0`), previous revisions (`v1.x`) are moved to `99-ARCHIVE/` with status changed to `DEPRECATED`.
- **Client Records:** Historical client project snapshots are retained for 3 years post-handover to support warranty inquiries and retrospective audits.
